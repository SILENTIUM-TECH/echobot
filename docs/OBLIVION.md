# Oblivion

← [Back to README](../README.md)

Oblivion is the assistant inside Echo. You don't need to learn commands: you say what you want, in your own words and your own language.

---

## What it can do

| Say | It does |
|---|---|
| "Oblivion, what's the time in Tokyo?" | Answers straight away |
| "Oblivion, translate this to Swahili" *(reply to a message)* | Translates it |
| "Oblivion, tell me about this image" *(send or reply to a photo)* | Describes it, reads any text in it, answers your question about it |
| "Oblivion, make this a sticker" | Runs the right tool for you |
| "Oblivion, close the group at 10pm" | Schedules it and confirms |
| "Oblivion, summarise today's chat" | Gives you the short version |

It understands context ("do that again"), remembers what you've told it, and answers in the language you write in.

---

## Turn it on

```text
.oblivion on          start answering in this chat
.oblivion off         stop
.oblivion status      see how it's set up here
.oblivion help        what it can do for you
```

Start a message with "Oblivion" or reply to one of its messages to talk to it. In groups, admins decide whether it's on.

---

## Make it yours

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.oblivion persona <style>` · `persona reset` | Give it a personality ("short and funny", "formal") |
| `.oblivion name <name>` | Rename it |
| `.oblivion lang <language>` | Pick a language |
| `.oblivion voice on\|off` | Reply with voice notes |
| `.oblivion timezone <city>` | So times make sense for you |
| `.oblivion remember <fact>` · `forget` · `memory` | Teach it, wipe it, or see what it knows |
| `.oblivion schedule list\|cancel` | See and manage things you asked it to do later |
| `.oblivion history` · `whoami` | Past activity and your permissions |

---

## Safe by design

- **It respects ranks.** Oblivion can only do what *you* are allowed to do. A member can't make it remove someone.
- **It asks first.** Removing members, closing groups or messaging many people needs your confirmation.
- **It stays private.** What you tell it is used to help you. `.oblivion forget` clears it.
- **It keeps working.** If one AI engine is busy, Oblivion switches to another, so replies keep flowing.

---

## For owners

`.oblivion logs`, `.oblivion report` and `.oblivion broadcast` show activity and send announcements. `.oblivion grant` and `revoke` control who may use it.
