## The idea

WhatsApp is where life happens, and it's full of small chores: tagging, moderating, converting, downloading, remembering, replying. Echo takes those off your plate so WhatsApp can go back to being about people.

## The maker

Echo is built and maintained by **Benjamin**, a young developer from Uganda in his early twenties. Online he's **Dev Aura** and **loner_01verified**, and he also goes by **𝐇𝐄'𝐒 𓊈∆𓊉 𝐃𝐄𝐕𖤐᭄𝕍𝕠𝕚𝔻⎯꯭ᴀᴜʀᴀ』** and **𝐋𝚯𝐍𝚵𝐑_𝟎𝟏**. He works under **Silentium Techies™**.

Echo started as a handful of commands for his own groups, and grew because the people around him kept asking for more. Today it's a full assistant with a dashboard, a Telegram companion and its own AI, still shaped by what real users ask for.

## What we care about

- **Calm over noise.** If a feature adds work, it isn't finished.
- **Your phone, your rules.** Echo is a linked device you can remove in one tap.
- **Honest limits.** We say what Echo can't do, and we fix what's broken fast.
- **Everyone's language.** From the commands to this documentation.

## Under the hood

Echo is built on the open-source **Baileys** library for WhatsApp's multi-device protocol, with a Node.js core, a web dashboard and a Telegram companion. See [How Echo works](ARCHITECTURE.md).
