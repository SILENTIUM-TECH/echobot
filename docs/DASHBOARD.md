# Dashboard

← [Back to README](../README.md)

**[echowabot.vercel.app](https://echowabot.vercel.app)**: your control room, in any browser or installed as an app on your phone.

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | See active sessions and messages at a glance, jump to anything with the search bar |
| **WhatsApp** | Add a session with a pairing code or QR, see who's connected, disconnect |
| **Shields** | Switch protections like anti-link and anti-view-once for your sessions |
| **Logs** | Watch live activity so you can see exactly what Echo did |
| **Settings** | Account, notifications, appearance |

---

## Meet your companion

A small friend lives on your dashboard. Pick one:

- **Male**
- **Female**
- **Robot**

They walk around, wave, sit, nap, celebrate your wins and chat. Each one speaks with a matching voice, and you control the size, volume and behaviour (or switch on reduced motion). Your choice is remembered across your devices.

---

## Install it like an app

Open the dashboard on your phone, tap *Add to Home screen*, and it behaves like a native app.
